Data-quality notes

    How to spot a mover

    • Blink: stars stay put; a dot that hops between epochs is moving.
    • Difference: static stars vanish; a mover leaves a dipole (dark/bright pair).
    • D6−D4: cold brown dwarfs are bright at 4.5 µm but dark in the 3.3 µm methane band.
    • No Gaia match? A moving source invisible to Gaia is too cold for optical light – exactly how volunteers find brown dwarfs.
    • Beware artifacts: ghosts and cosmic rays appear in one frame only, or move with the detector instead of the sky.
